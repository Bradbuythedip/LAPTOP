What these numbers are. For constant-product pools (Uniswap V2, Aerodrome volatile) the output is exact — it is the same formula the pool itself runs. For concentrated-liquidity pools (Uniswap V3, Slipstream) it is an estimate that assumes the pool's current liquidity extends across your whole trade. Real pools run out of liquidity at range boundaries this tool does not read, and when that happens you get less than shown. Treat the V3 numbers as a best case, and treat a large trade in a new pool as very likely worse than shown.

What is not included. Gas. MEV. The pool changing between this reading and your transaction — on launch day that is the norm, not the exception. Any hook on a v4 pool that taxes or blocks the swap. Price impact is not the same as loss: it is what you pay to move the price, and you pay it again in reverse when you sell.
