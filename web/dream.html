The one number

Hold one $SNOOZE untouched for ninety days and you have accrued exactly one $DREAM. Both tokens have nine decimals, so that is a count and not a conversion — an equivalent token, for doing nothing.

The ramp is quadratic, and almost everybody reads it as linear. Half the time is a quarter of the reward, not half: day 45 is 0.25, not 0.5. The first week is worth six-thousandths of your bag. It is back-loaded on purpose — the reward is for the ninetieth day, not the first.

What breaks a streak

Any outbound transfer. Any.

A sale, a move to your own second wallet, an exchange deposit, sending a friend one base unit. From inside a transfer those are the same event, and no attempt is made to tell them apart — a rule that trusted you about which was which would be farmed with two wallets before lunch.

What you lose is the rate, not the bank.

Everything accrued up to that moment is banked and stays claimable forever. The clock goes back to zero, so you start again at the bottom of the ramp. A ninety-day streak is worth twice a fresh one per day held, and that gap is the whole mechanism.

What does not break it.

Receiving more $SNOOZE. Claiming your $DREAM — nothing leaves your wallet, so there is nothing to break. Selling $DREAM itself. And buying more rides the clock you already have, on the whole bag, which is why adding to a streaked wallet beats starting a fresh one.

Emptying the wallet ends it properly.

Sell everything and buy back three months later and you start from zero, not from where you left off. A wallet holding none of the token has no streak to resume.

What $DREAM is not

Not capped.

After the ninety-day ramp the rate is flat, not zero: another two per $SNOOZE every ninety days, for as long as anybody holds. Emission never stops and there is no supply ceiling. Anyone describing $DREAM as scarce is describing a different token.

Not backed, and not a claim on anything.

No ETH behind it, no treasury, no redemption, no share of fees, no claim on the curve or the pool. The contract mints a count. It cannot mint a bid, and nothing here can tell you what one will be worth — including zero.

Not free to everybody else.

Rule 3's bookkeeping runs inside every $SNOOZE transfer, so every holder pays a little more gas on every transfer whether or not they ever claim. Measured at about 57,000 execution gas for a wallet-to-wallet send.

Not for the pool, and not for the launcher.

Registered pools accrue nothing, and neither does the one cap-exempt owner wallet. The launcher cannot farm the reward for holding its own float. That is checkable: capExempt() reads true for both, on chain, forever.

Not switched on until somebody names it.

The reward token is set once, before freeze(), and can never be repointed. If it is never set, accrual still runs and nobody can ever mint against it — a number on chain and nothing more. Check dream() before you believe any of this.

None of this is a reason to expect a price. Rules 1 and 2 slow an exit and Rule 3 pays for staying, and all three together do not make anybody want the token. They are plumbing. Read the two rules for what the other two actually do, which is less than they sound like.
