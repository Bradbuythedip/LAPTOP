Nothing here is deployed and nothing is for sale. This page is a simulator: it does arithmetic on parameters you type and shows what a deployment would look like. There is no wallet connection, no contract, no token, and nothing to buy.
POT PAL is not affiliated with LAPTOP or with laptoptoken.com, and is not endorsed by them or by anyone depicted in any artwork. It is an unrelated memecoin that happens to be an anagram. Quoted against ETH — not paired against LAPTOP, so it can never appear in a LAPTOP pool listing and be mistaken for one.

What the checks are. Uniswap V2 stores pool reserves as uint112, so a supply that seems merely silly can be literally impossible to pool. Uniswap V3 and v4 represent price as a tick bounded at ±887272, so an extreme ratio of tokens to ETH can fall outside the range a pool can express at all. Both limits are protocol facts, not opinions, and both are checked above against the numbers you typed.

What this page does not do. It does not deploy, sign, broadcast, or hold anything. It reads nothing live — the ETH price is whatever you type, which is why every fiat figure here is labelled as resting on it. It is not advice to launch a token, and a token being deployable is not a statement that deploying it is a good idea.
